Questions

Good to know

Do I need to pay for Google Sheets?

No. Google Sheets is free with any Google account.

How do I get my sheet after I buy it?

You'll get an email right away with a link. Click it, press "Make a copy," and the sheet is yours.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, in the free Google Sheets app. It's easiest to set up on a computer first.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You type in your own numbers, so your bank login stays private.
