Money should feel simple

budget website started with one personal budget sheet and a simple goal: make it easy to see where your money goes.

A lot of budgeting tools are either too complicated, too expensive or want your bank login. We build the opposite: clean, simple tools that do the math for you and stay out of your way.

Today that means Google Sheets budget templates for every level, and a budgeting app that's free to use with a Pro upgrade for bigger goals.

What we believe

  • Simple beats fancy. If it takes more than five minutes to start, it's too complicated.
  • Your data is yours. No bank logins, and we never sell your information.
  • Budgeting isn't only for experts. Our tools work for any income and any starting point.
