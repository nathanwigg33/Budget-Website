How can we help?

Answers to common questions about the budget sheets and the app.

Budget sheets

How do I get my sheet after I buy it?

Right after checkout you'll get an email with a link. Open it, press "Make a copy" and the sheet is saved to your own Google Drive.

I didn't get my email.

Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it's not there, contact us below with the email you used at checkout and we'll resend it.

Do I need to pay for Google Sheets?

No. Google Sheets is free with any Google account.

Can I use it in Excel?

The sheets are built for Google Sheets. Some features, like certain charts, may not work the same in Excel.

Can I customize the categories?

Yes. You can rename, add or remove categories. The Complete and Ultimate sheets have a Settings tab to make this easy.

Can I share my sheet with my partner?

Yes, you can share your own copy with people in your household. Please don't share the purchase link publicly.

Budget app

Is the app free?

Yes. The core budgeting features are free. Pro adds net worth tracking, goals, reports and more.

Does the app connect to my bank?

No. You enter your own numbers, so you never share your bank login.
